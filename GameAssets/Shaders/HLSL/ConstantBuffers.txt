Constant buffers:
    - (0) Globals: projection, view, resolution, time, mouse
    - (1) Light: Light direction, color, etc.
    - (2) Color: Color.
    - (3) Transform: model, normal matrices.
    - (4) Bones: Bone transforms and normal transforms.
    - (5) Outline: Outline uniforms: width, JFAlevel
    - (6) Kernel: Kernel matrix.
    - (7) Text: Pen and size.
    - (8) Tesselation: Amount and padding.
    - (9) Sea: Wind and dimensions
    - (10) FFT: N, direction, stage
